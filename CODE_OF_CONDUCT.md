# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and keep
discussion focused on improving the project. Harassment, discriminatory
language, threats, doxxing, and sexualized conduct directed at community members
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report
conduct issues privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
